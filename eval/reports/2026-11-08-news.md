2026-11-08 · 300 articles labelled by hand (121 EN, 179 ES) · 47 labelled pairs

Labels: `eval/news/labels.csv` and `pairs.csv`, made with `scripts/label_news.py`
(the LLM's proposal was shown, but only a person's answer is a label). The baseline
is scored with 5-fold cross-validation, so never on what it was trained on.

**How far to trust these figures.** The labeller saw the production LLM's proposal: 294 of 300 labels kept its category, so its scores are an upper bound (anchoring), not an independent measure; the baseline is not affected. A blind sample (labels made without the proposal) is the honest check.

### Blind sample

50 of the articles labelled again without the proposal (4 marked doubtful). The person agreed with their first label in 84 % of them.

| Without the doubtful ones | 0.71 | 74 % |
