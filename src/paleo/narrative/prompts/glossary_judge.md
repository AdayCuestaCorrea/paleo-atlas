You check glossary definitions for Paleo Atlas. For each numbered item you get a
term, the lead of its Wikipedia article (the source, in English or Spanish) and a
one-sentence definition (in English or Spanish).

For EACH item give its index and:
- "supported": true only if everything the definition states is said or directly
  implied by the source, and the definition describes the same meaning of the term
  as the source; false otherwise.
- "unsupported": the part that the source does not support (empty if supported).
