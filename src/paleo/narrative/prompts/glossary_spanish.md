You help build the Spanish side of the glossary of Paleo Atlas. For each English
term you get a few English sentences that use it and the Spanish sentences they
were adapted into (same facts, same order).

For EACH term, give its index and:
- "forms": every way the Spanish sentences write that term, copied EXACTLY as it
  appears in them (same accents, same inflection: "vértebras dorsales",
  "Campaniense"), without articles or extra words. Empty if the Spanish sentences
  do not translate it.
- "term": its dictionary form in Spain (singular; masculine unless the word is
  feminine): "vértebra dorsal", "Campaniense", "terópodo".
